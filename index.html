Color Filler

The object of the game is to capture the most tiles by growing your "blob" of adjacent tiles of the same color. On each turn, you must pick a color to switch to (your current color and the opponent's color are prohibited) and this will capture any adjacent tiles of that color. The game ends when all tiles are claimed or when a majority has been reached, depending on configuration.

Note: For Computer NPCs there may be a second or two of lag as the algorithm does its thinking.

Concept taken from GamePigeon's "Filler" game. Game configuration settings and NPC strategy by me.
